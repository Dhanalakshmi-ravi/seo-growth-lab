CORE CONCEPTS

Understanding Off-Page SEO

Backlinks

A backlink is a link from another website pointing to your website.

Link Relevance

Relevant links can provide useful context and referral traffic.

Outreach

Outreach involves contacting relevant website owners or publishers with useful resources.

Digital PR

Creating useful information and stories that may earn attention and links from publishers.

MY SEO EXPERIMENT

Planned Link-Building Workflow

1. Research relevant websites.

2. Identify useful resource pages.

3. Analyze competitor backlink opportunities.

4. Create a useful resource.

5. Prepare personalized outreach.

6. Track outreach and responses.

7. Evaluate referral traffic and link quality.
